# Verify first owners independently and recover cases only on request

Accepted decision from Chief and Dot, 2026-10-17. The initial cohort's ownership is manually approved from independently verified business control and authority, with recorded approvals and transfers; public listings, RFC/CLEE and supplied contacts are not proof. Existing buyers receive access recovery only when they request it and original contact evidence is trustworthy, rather than through an automatic campaign that might reach a contact altered through formerly open writes.
