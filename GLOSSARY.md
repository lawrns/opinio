# Opinio — access and authority

Opinio pairs public merchant passports with buyer-led case resolution. Existing widget and score language remains in CONTEXT.md; these terms define access to private business and buyer work.

## Language

**Buyer**:
The person who opened a resolution case and can explicitly confirm whether its remedy resolved their problem.
_Avoid_: Account holder, merchant customer account.

**Resolution Case**:
The record of one buyer's problem with a business and the subsequent remedy conversation.
_Avoid_: Login account, purchase proof.

**Buyer Confirmation**:
The buyer's explicit statement that a case was resolved to their satisfaction. A merchant's remedy assertion is separate.
_Avoid_: Automatic resolution, merchant confirmation.

**Business Membership**:
An approved person's active authority to perform a defined role for a particular business.
_Avoid_: Claimed listing, verified RFC, public profile ownership.

**Owner**:
A business member authorized to manage that business's staff and access. Initial ownership requires independently verified business control and authority.
_Avoid_: First visitor, first claimant.

**Staff**:
A business member invited by an owner to carry out the operational work granted by their role.
_Avoid_: Public user, owner by default.

**Ownership Approval**:
The recorded decision granting first-owner or transferred-owner authority after independently verifying business control and the person's authority.
_Avoid_: Profile claim, identifier lookup.

**Case Access Link**:
A private, time-limited invitation to prove access to one buyer's case. Opening it is not buyer confirmation.
_Avoid_: Review invitation, public case link.

**Recovery**:
Buyer-requested restoration of access to an existing case using independently supported original contact evidence.
_Avoid_: Bulk reactivation, retroactive campaign.

**Review Invitation**:
A request to leave feedback about a business; recording it does not establish delivery or authority to a buyer's case.
_Avoid_: Case access link, authentication credential.

**Machine Key**:
A revocable business credential that grants only the named integration operations for one business.
_Avoid_: Merchant Widget Token, buyer link, shared merchant password.
