# Strategic SEO & Organic Growth Plan for Opinio.mx

**Domain**: `https://opinio.mx`  
**Market**: Mexico (es-MX)  
**Catalog Size**: 1,197 E-Commerce Stores + 5 Verified Anchor Brands  
**Primary Keywords**: "¿Es confiable [Marca]?", "¿Es seguro comprar en [Marca]?", "Quejas [Marca] PROFECO", "Opiniones [Marca] México"  

---

## 1. High-Intent Keyword Strategy & Query Mechanics
In the Mexican e-commerce ecosystem, consumer trust is the primary conversion friction point due to prevalent transfer fraud and unverified social media merchants.

Search query volume concentrates heavily on pre-purchase validation patterns:
1. **Validation Queries**:
   - `es confiable [tienda]` (e.g. `es confiable tane`, `es confiable luuna`)
   - `es seguro comprar en [tienda]`
   - `quejas [tienda] profeco`
2. **Reputación y Experiencias**:
   - `opiniones de [tienda]`
   - `reseñas reales [tienda]`
3. **Identidad Fiscal y Aclaraciones**:
   - `rfc de [tienda]`
   - `razon social [tienda]`
   - `telefono atencion a clientes [tienda]`

---

## 2. Programmatic Architecture & Internal Linking
- **Homepage (`/`)**: High authority brand hub, Sitelinks SearchBox Schema (`WebSite`), links to categories and featured merchants.
- **Full Directory Index (`/directorio`)**: Server-side rendered collection of all 1,197 stores categorized into 8 core verticals. Crawlers discover 100% of store URLs within 2 clicks.
- **Search & Verification (`/verificar`)**: Interactive client search engine with real-time filtering by rating, category, and order verification.
- **Store Passports (`/b/[slug]`)**: 1,197 programmatic landing pages with server-rendered JSON-LD (`Organization`, `AggregateRating`, `Review`, `BreadcrumbList`, `WebPage`), OpenGraph assets, and citable GEO blocks.
